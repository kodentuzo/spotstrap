Put `switch` on an `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">`. Use it for a setting that takes
effect immediately; use a checkbox in a form that's submitted.

<Example name="switch/basic" title="Switches" />

## Classes

| Class | What |
| --- | --- |
| `switch` | The track and thumb, 51 × 31, with a 44px tall hit area. |
| `choice` | A 44px row with the switch and its label. |

It styles the same checked, focus and disabled states as [Checkbox](/components/checkbox). With
reduced motion, the thumb moves without animating.
