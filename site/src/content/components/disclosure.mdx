Give several `<details>` the same `name` and they open one at a time. Where the browser can
animate `<details>`, it opens smoothly.

<Example name="disclosure/accordion" title="An accordion" minHeight={220} />

## Classes

| Class | What |
| --- | --- |
| `disclosure`, `disclosure-button`, `disclosure-panel` | The section, its 44px summary row and its content. |

| State | Attributes |
| --- | --- |
| Open | `details[open]`, or on a library's button: `[aria-expanded="true"]`, `[data-state="open"]`, `[data-open]` |
