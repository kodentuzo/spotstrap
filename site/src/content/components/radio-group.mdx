Put `radio` on each `<input type="radio">` and `radio-group` on their container. A `fieldset`
with a `legend` names the group.

<Example name="radio-group/basic" title="A radio group" minHeight={220} />

## Classes

| Class | What |
| --- | --- |
| `radio-group` | Spaces the rows 12px apart. |
| `choice` | A 44px row with the radio and its label. |
| `radio` | The circle, with a 44 × 44 hit area. |

It styles the same states as [Checkbox](/components/checkbox).
